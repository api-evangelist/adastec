# ADASTEC Corp.

> ADASTEC Corp. is an automated-driving software company that builds flowride.ai, an SAE Level-4
> automated driving software and sensor platform for full-size commercial vehicles — factory-fitted
> electric buses and minibuses running pre-mapped routes in mixed public-road traffic. Headquarters
> is East Lansing, Michigan, with offices in Helmond (Netherlands) and Istanbul (Turkey).

## Platform

flowride.ai is organized in three parts, per the product page:

- flowride Drive — the on-vehicle Level-4 automation stack plus an HMI connector.
- flowride Cloud — central control, data storage, data sharing, and what the company describes as
  an Open API. No contract for it is published.
- flowride Apps — remote management, command and control, a passenger application, and analytics.

Operating design domain, as published: predetermined and pre-mapped routes, mixed traffic,
multiple stops, day and night operation, rain and haze, controllable maximum speed of 35 mph,
no safety driver on the route. Sensor stack includes Ouster LiDAR, Teledyne FLIR thermal cameras,
GNSS, automotive radar and ultrasonic sensors.
